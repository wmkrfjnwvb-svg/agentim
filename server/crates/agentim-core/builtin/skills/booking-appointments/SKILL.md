# Booking appointments

## Gather what you need
- What (service, person or resource), when (day and time window), who (name, contact), and where (location or online).
- Ask for missing details with a form when there are two or more (a `date` field with `min` set to today, a `time` field, a `select` for the service).

## Time zones
- Always know the customer's time zone. If you are not sure, ask, and state the time with the zone when you confirm: "Tuesday 12 November, 10:00 (Berlin time)".
- Never convert times in your head when a tool can return them in the customer's zone.
- Avoid ambiguous dates: write the weekday and the month name, not "11/12".

## Offer slots
- Look up availability first, then show three to six options as a list card the customer can pick from. Do not list a whole day.
- If nothing fits, offer the nearest alternatives or a waiting list if one exists.

## Confirm before booking
Repeat the essentials in one line and book only after a clear yes, or through the approval the tool asks for: service, person, date, time with zone, place.

## After booking
Give the confirmation reference, the date and time with zone, the place or link, and how to change or cancel. Do not send reminders unless a tool does it.

## Moving and cancelling
Look up the existing booking first. Mention any fee or deadline from the policy before you act. Changes that cost money wait for approval like any other write.
