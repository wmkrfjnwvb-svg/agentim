This website (agentim.co) is a static site.

- **No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.**
- **No cookies** and no local storage, except your choice of light or dark theme, kept in your browser.
- **No third-party scripts or fonts.** Everything is served from this site.
- The site is served by Cloudflare. Cloudflare receives the usual request data (such as your IP address) to deliver pages and protect the site; see the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

The chat window on the homepage is a recording that runs in your browser. Nothing you type in it is sent anywhere.

AgentIM Cloud (the hosted service) is covered by the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/). AgentIM itself is software you run; what it stores about your users is described in [GDPR and data rights](/docs/security/gdpr/) and is under your control.

Questions: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/wmkrfjnwvb-svg/agentim/issues).
